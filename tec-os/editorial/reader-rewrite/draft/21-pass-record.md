## Corrections and qualifications

- I replaced the old blanket successful-persistence sentence with the accepted
  warm-flush boundary. Failed lines are dropped and Write lost clears the new
  or old chain before the next command load. Successful syntax is not proof
  that the chained command will run.
- The old “only on ^C” pager wording is replaced by the exact masked-key rule,
  preserving both aliases: # drops the chain, while semicolon stops only the
  listing, like Esc. The Q/q distinction and both carry-return paths remain.
- The old absolute workspace locations are preserved as historical values,
  without silently generating or claiming a new qualified map.
- The replacement-command source lifetime is caller memory that a subsequent
  load may overwrite. This does not license overwriting resident workspace.
- The prose uses the new chapter numbers12,20,25 and28. Navigation/anchors still
  require whole-book integration. Rootbook and shared ledgers are unchanged.
